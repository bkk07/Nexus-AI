# Employee Benefits & Workplace Policy Guide (2026)

## 1. Remote Work & Learning Allowance
Full-time employees receive two annual equipment and education stipends:

* **Home Office Budget:** $1,200 USD per calendar year for monitors, ergonomic seating, and peripherals. Reimbursed within 14 business days upon submitting receipts via Expensify.
* **Learning & Conference Budget:** $2,500 USD per year covering books, online courses, and technical conference tickets (requires prior approval from your direct manager).

## 2. Leave & Paid Time Off (PTO)
* **Standard PTO:** 22 days per calendar year accrued on a bi-weekly basis.
* **Sick Leave:** 10 fully paid sick days per year (no medical note required for absences under 3 consecutive days).
* **PTO Rollover:** Up to 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into the following year. Any remaining unused balance beyond 5 days expires on December 31st without cash payout.

## 3. Health & Wellness Stipend
Employees are eligible for a monthly $100 USD wellness reimbursement covering gym memberships, mental health apps, or athletic equipment.
